# Code of Conduct

Chronoglyph adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

Harassment, trolling, personal attacks, publishing private information, sexualized conduct, and other behavior inappropriate in a professional setting are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and when someone officially represents the project. Maintainers may remove or reject contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

For a confidential report, contact the maintainer using the contact method listed on the [jiaoqsh GitHub profile](https://github.com/jiaoqsh). Do not disclose sensitive conduct reports in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with respect for the reporter's privacy and security.

The Contributor Covenant 2.1 community impact guidelines govern enforcement decisions.
